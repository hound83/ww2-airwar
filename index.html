What is historical, and what are we estimating?

Documented monthly pilot losses SOURCE

Williamson Murray provides 1943 fighter-pilot loss figures. The model anchors aggregate loss probability to those figures, including wounded, killed and missing pilots.

1942 & 1944 loss curves ESTIMATE

Murray's scanned Table LXVIII contains January 1942–May 1944 pilot losses, but its graph has not been fully transcribed. Editable placeholders remain clearly flagged.

Victory-record research SOURCE

Ager and colleagues study 5,081 German fighter pilots with at least one victory. Their sample excludes pilots who never scored; our all-pilot averages therefore remain model estimates.

Primary and research references

  • Williamson Murray, Strategy for Defeat — complete digitized scan (see printed pages 310–311, Table LXVIII).
  • Murray, conclusion and surviving pilot-attrition discussion.
  • Ager et al., Killer Incentives, 2021 — German fighter-pilot victory records and career histories.
  • Murray, 1943 pilot loss totals and unit strength.
Interpretation: This is an illustrative Monte Carlo-style agent simulation, not a reconstruction of actual individual Luftwaffe personnel. There is no verified complete roster, no all-pilot victory database, and no historical Luftwaffe-wide average sorties/engagements for this exact period. The app is designed for sensitivity analysis, not for reporting invented precision as fact.
