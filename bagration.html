22 June–29 August 1944

Operation Bagration

Four Soviet fronts broke through Army Group Centre in Belarus and drove west. Their air armies covered crossings, struck retreat routes and moved to forward fields. German records follow the Luftwaffe’s withdrawal and the changing strength of individual formations.
