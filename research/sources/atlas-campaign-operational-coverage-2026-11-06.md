# Atlas campaign progression and source-scope audit, 6 November 2026

## Purpose and method

The v8 review preview previously offered source-backed operational phases for only five of the 20 named campaigns. A user could select many other campaigns and find a general introduction followed by an isolated date, without any explanation of the military sequence. This increment expands seven additional campaigns to source-backed, navigable phases and improves the event-level evidence in the original five. Phase boundaries are **editorial reading guides** based on official published histories; they are not independent numerical reporting periods or exact aircraft deployments.

The canonical page records, source URLs, populations, date ranges and caveats remain in `dist/grand-air-war-v77-campaigns.js`. The following is the human-readable source trail for the *new* operational research. Source labels in the dataset are cited by their keys in parentheses.

## Previously phased campaigns whose evidence was strengthened

- **Spain / Condor Legion:** source-supported dated episodes remain within the existing three phases; Bundesarchiv and RAF Museum information is historical context, not a monthly German strength register.
- **Poland 1939:** established three-stage dossier remains the initial deep pilot; the September day records must be read as individual operations, not verified all-front Polish or German air-loss totals. See `poland-1939-campaign-pilot-2026-11-05.md`.
- **France 1940:** phase-appropriate event detail added, retaining original RAF Museum and RAF Air Power Review observations with their own dates and reporting scopes.
- **Battle of Britain:** specific convoy *Peewit* (8 August), *Adlertag* (13 August) and London shift (7 September) are now searchable and clickable. See RAF Museum phases (`rafBritainPhases`, `rafBritainChannel`, `rafBritainAirfields`, `rafBritainBlitz`). RAF victory claims remain distinct from captured German loss returns.
- **Normandy 1944:** Overlord, Cobra and Falaise dates linked back to RAF Museum, National Museum USAF and US Army histories (`rafNormandyOperations`, `usafNormandyCobra`, `usArmyNormandyFalaise`). These are not evidence for reconstructing every unit's exact map position or aircraft losses.

Two short operations still have **researched event dossiers without synthetic phase sequences**: Dieppe (19 August 1942) and Bodenplatte (1 January 1945). The Netherlands Institute for Military History gives about **175** aircraft at Eindhoven, **45 destroyed** and **35 damaged** there on 1 January. Those local figures must not be added to the RAF Museum's whole-raid **224 destroyed** and **84 damaged beyond repair**: geographic subsets overlap, and the damaged categories are not defined identically (`ninhEindhoven1945`, `raf1945`).

## Explicit conflicting sources

**Malta, Operation Spotter, 7 March 1942.** The RAF Museum 1942 chronology associates the arrival of **15 Spitfires** with USS *Wasp*, while Imperial War Museums photograph A 7840 identifies **HMS *Eagle*** and a captioned load of **16**. The page presents the disagreement openly in the phase limits and does not silently resolve ship or shipped/arrived denominator. The original carrier operational log, RAF delivery returns, and IWM metadata would be necessary for final reconciliation.

## Audit status and remaining research

- Added material was cross-checked against institutional museum and military-history publications and their linked context. **Original mission returns and each original scan have not been independently re-transcribed** as part of this milestone. A museum account is not the original wartime ledger.
- All 37 authored phases now link to at least one inspected, source-described date. This is a *coverage guarantee*, not proof every day has been documented.
- **Six** of the 20 campaigns still have no source-local numerical observation: Stalingrad, defence of the Reich, Kursk, Italian campaign, Bagration and Berlin. Eight campaigns remain without reviewed multi-stage histories. These gaps are visibly preserved; neither other theatres' returns nor unrelated months are substituted as zero.
- Front/command German losses, VVS front-plus-reserve strength, ETO/MTO USAAF group strength and worldwide factory acceptances remain separate research populations and are **not** made campaign-local by clicking a map marker.
