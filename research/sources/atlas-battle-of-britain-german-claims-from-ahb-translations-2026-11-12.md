# Battle of Britain — German claims extracted from RAF AHB translations (12 November 2026)

Extraction of Luftwaffe claims of enemy aircraft from the RAF Air Historical Branch translations of the Luftwaffe Führungsstab IC situation reports (translations 122 and 123). These cover 14 July–15 August 1940. Claims are as recorded in the contemporary German reports; they are not verified against RAF losses.

## Cumulative survey (1 July–15 August) — already noted
From situation report 345 (15 August):
- By fighters: 670
- By AA: 76
- On ground: 77
- **Total confirmed: 823**
- Of which fighters (Spitfire/Hurricane/Curtiss/Defiant): 574

## Additional German intelligence estimate (end of July)
From situation report 330 (1 August), “Appreciation of the Effect of Air Attacks on Great Britain to the Present Date”:
- In July: 166 fighters shot down.
- With damage and other causes: total enemy losses for July estimated at 220–250 fighters.
- Replacements estimated at ~220 theoretical / 180 practical.

## Status
- Daily German claim entries for 14 July–15 August are now extracted from the primary AHB translations.
- The 1 July–15 August cumulative survey (823) is confirmed in the same source.
- Coverage ends on 15 August in the available translations (Parts 1–3). Later parts covering late August–October are not listed as separate AHB translations on the RAF site and remain open.
- Claims are contemporary German figures and should be kept distinct from verified RAF losses.

## Atlas implication
A partial German claim series (mid-July to mid-August) is now available for review against the existing Churchill weekly German loss series and the Atlas 17-week structure. The full campaign German claim series still requires the later situation reports or an alternative primary source.
