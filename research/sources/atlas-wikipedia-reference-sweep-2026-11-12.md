## Sources that could supply the largest additions

1. **Allied bombing operations.** The Davis study's appendices identify seven workbooks, the sheet key and fields covering target, bomb load, aircraft attacking and aircraft lost. The listed scope includes RAF Bomber Command, RAF 205 Group and covered US heavy bomber commands. The book opens. The tested 1944 workbook and key returned access errors. The coverage file preserves all seven exact URLs. Recover the sheet key and workbook, then reconcile one month with an existing Atlas series before expanding. [Study and workbook index](https://media.defense.gov/2017/Mar/31/2001725263/-1/-1/0/B_0099_DAVIS_BOMBING_AXIS_POWERS.PDF)
2. **German air fleet and formation returns.** CAMO collection 500, inventory 12452 identifies 678 files, including records of the Luftflotten and flying units. File 427 is a bounded Luftflotte 4 operations and losses lead for July 1941. The archive metadata was accessible. Aircraft counts still require document-level transcription. [OKL catalogue](https://wwii.germandocsinrussia.org/de/nodes/2410-opis-12452-glavnoe-komandovanie-lyuftvaffe-okl) · [File 427](https://wwii.germandocsinrussia.org/de/nodes/2713)
3. **Cobra.** Frappé's revised work and the 2nd TAF operations record book remain mission-level leads. The accessible sources supply catalogue and citation information. Use the original US escort annex and German dated unit returns to match encounters. [Frappé catalogue record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb45702024v) · [CARL Missions 492 and 494](https://cgsc.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/collection/p4013coll8/id/4574/)
