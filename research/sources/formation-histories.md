# Formation and airfield histories

The [reader index](../../formations.html) follows 26 distinct wartime formations in six flagship campaigns. One of them continues into the Berlin campaign. Each dated entry and station period in the [transcription](../transcriptions/formation-histories.json) identifies its source and passage. The selection includes fighters, bombers, reconnaissance, ground crews and higher formations. Parent and subordinate units sometimes describe the same operation.

## Source decisions

The 82 dated entries and 41 named operating stations use 44 linked sources. The station index covers departure bases and documented transfer destinations, with the status given beside each place. The unit articles name operational localities separately. Some dates are available only to a month or period. Campaign membership records a documented role. Counts in the table overlap when a unit served in multiple campaigns.

- **Sicilian shipping cover.** The [squadron Form 540](https://www.92-squadron.com/copy-of-form-540-oct-1941-sept-1942) names Nelson and Rodney on 16 July. The [Nelson service chronology](https://www.naval-history.net/xGM-Chrono-01BB-Nelson.htm) puts Rodney’s reunion with Force H on the 16th and Nelson’s bombardment after the torpedoing of Indomitable early on the 17th. We retained the patrol, its size and its reported beneficiaries and revised the existing campaign case. The timing of both ships’ gunfire and any prevention of aircraft losses remain open.
- **Ponte Olivo and Pachino.** The RAF campaign history, NATAF appendices and squadron diary describe capture, unit arrival, operational status and actual sorties on different dates. A patrol from Malta that ended in Sicily is explicitly recorded as such. Follow the [Ponte Olivo group](../../formations.html#unit-us-31fg) and [Pachino wing](../../formations.html#unit-raf-244).
- **Malta deliveries.** Hague and the RAF count 15 arrivals for Spotter while IWM’s photograph caption gives 16. The [Spotter case](../../malta.html#spotter) preserves both. Calendar’s 46 arrivals belong to the joint operation. They cannot be allocated between Nos. 601 and 603 with the material used here.
- **Normandy effects.** Photographs, pilot observations, concentrated bombing patterns and ground-unit occupation have different meanings. The 416th reported no attack at Marigny while the 384th flew its Caen sortie without releasing. Neither nullifies the wider force’s activity. The PR #5 station candidate is useful for opening-day context. Dated group histories and mission reports supply the station periods and effects printed here.
- **Bagration returns.** A German monthly inventory can bound a comparison without identifying an individual combat. The 1st Air Army study prints a loss headline of 119 and components totalling 179. Both remain visible. No. 310’s wing claims, the 445th’s contribution to an Eighth Air Force total and the Bobruisk army totals likewise represent overlapping reporting levels.
