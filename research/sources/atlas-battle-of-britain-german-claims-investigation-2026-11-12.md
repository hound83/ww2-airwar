# Battle of Britain — investigation of the open German claim series (12 November 2026)

Investigation of the remaining gap for milestone 1: a consistent German claim series (Luftwaffe claims of RAF aircraft) across the 17 weeks / five phases, as distinct from the German loss series already extracted.

## What is available

### Campaign-scale total
Multiple secondary sources cite **3,058 Luftwaffe claims** against **915 RAF fighter losses** for the full 10 July–31 October period (already noted in the Atlas baseline). Over-claiming ratio is approximately 3.3 : 1, compared with the RAF’s roughly 1.6 : 1 over-claiming against German losses (2,692/2,698 claims vs 1,733 recorded German losses).

### Partial intelligence survey (1 July–15 August)
Luftwaffe situation report No. 345 (operations of 15 August) contains a “Survey of enemy losses from 1 July to 15 August 1940”:
- Confirmed by fighters: 670
- By AA: 76
- On ground: 77
- **Total confirmed: 823**
- Of which fighters (Spitfire/Hurricane/Curtiss/Defiant): 574
- Additional estimated losses due to other causes: 196 → total 770 fighters
- Replacement estimate 270–300 → remaining first-line fighters ~430, of which ~300 serviceable.
This is a partial window only and is an intelligence estimate, not a pure claim list.

### Selected daily German claims (intensive days)
- 13 August: German claim of 132 British aircraft destroyed (secondary citations).
- 15 August: German high command claimed 99 RAF fighters destroyed in the air (Air & Space Forces Magazine / secondary).
- Other intensive days have isolated contemporary claims but no systematic daily or weekly series in open sources.

### Primary source location
RAF Air Historical Branch translations of the Luftwaffe Führungsstab IC situation reports for the Battle of Britain period exist and are listed on the RAF site:
- Part 1: 30 June–13 July 1940 (translation 119)
- Part 2: 14–31 July 1940 (translation 122)
- Part 3: 1–15 August 1940 (translation 123)
These are the logical primary source for a systematic German claim extraction. They were not newly transcribed in this pass.

## What is still missing
- A consistent weekly or phase-level German claim series covering the full 10 July–31 October period that can be placed next to the Churchill weekly German loss series and the Atlas 17-week structure.
- Unit-level claim lists or daily claim aggregates for the majority of the 114 days.
- Clear separation of German fighter claims vs bomber/AA claims across the whole campaign.

## Atlas implication
The reverse comparison can currently be built with:
- German losses (weekly Churchill series + Hansard phase totals) — available.
- RAF losses (weekly Hurricane/Spitfire + campaign 915) — available.
- German claims — only the campaign total (3,058) and one partial intelligence survey (823 to 15 August) plus scattered daily figures.

A full weekly German claim series remains the principal open item for a complete 17-week reverse model.

## Recommended next step
Transcribe the RAF AHB translations of the Luftwaffe Führungsstab IC situation reports (translations 119, 122, 123 and any later parts covering September–October) for daily or weekly claim aggregates. That is the highest-value primary source still open.

Sources: Churchill / World Wars.net; Hansard 14 May 1947; Luftwaffe situation report 345 via battle-of-britain-diary.org.uk; RAF AHB German translations list; secondary citations in Air & Space Forces Magazine and historyofwar.org; existing Atlas notes.
