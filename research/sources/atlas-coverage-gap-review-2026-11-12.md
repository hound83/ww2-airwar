# Atlas coverage, gaps and challenge review — 12 November 2026

Single-pass review of current research state, highest-leverage gaps, challenge observations, and recommended next actions. Performed under the new challenge and joint-review role.

## 1. Current coverage snapshot

Strongest areas already in the Atlas or research ledger:

- **Battle of Britain**: 17 weekly and 5 phase comparisons with leave-one-out validation (average miss 16.5 on weekly claims). Seven reverse dated cases. Source hierarchy and period discrepancies preserved.
- **Normandy 1–18 July and Cobra**: Paired Allied/German reporting directions, 18-day series, sensitivity on Cobra (approx. 8 from 12 claims, 7–9 range), inventory ceilings, and explicit cause/service coverage limits.
- **Regional and campaign bilateral checks**: Selected 1944 dates, Bobruisk inventory ceiling, Dieppe, Schweinfurt, and broader 20-campaign source audit adding 343 rows.
- **German AHB returns**: Multiple Luftflotte Reich and West month-end totals transcribed and visually verified (including corrections for overwritten figures). Type-level June candidates remain provisional.
- **Source audit across 20 campaigns**: Reference recheck of 143 source IDs, recovery of alternate copies, and documented remaining archival leads.

The live Atlas already distinguishes claims from verified losses, preserves reporting scope, and surfaces source discrepancies rather than averaging them.

## 2. Highest-leverage gaps

For the planned **January–June 1944** operational dataset and for tightening existing Atlas content:

- Mission-level attribution for German losses against specific Allied raids (especially Cobra / Mission 494 fighter annex and airframe matching). Current calculators correctly flag wider command scope.
- Consistent German weekly or phase claim series for Battle of Britain reverse direction (currently only campaign-scale and seven dated cases).
- Original daily or unit-level returns for Eastern Front and Mediterranean that would allow tighter reciprocal checks beyond secondary extracts.
- Type- and unit-complete reconciliation of the provisional June 1944 Bf 109 / Fw 190 OCR candidates against month-end totals.
- Coverage gaps explicitly noted in the campaign audit (e.g., full Mission 494 annex, certain TsAMO files, original AIR 27/50 for held-out BoB dates).

These gaps limit how far claim-to-loss ratios can be pushed without additional primary matching.

## 3. Challenge notes

- The v9.0 leave-one-out estimators are useful diagnostic tools, but the phase-level errors (average miss 59) and the fact that ranges are derived from other periods in the same campaign mean they should not be presented as predictive for new periods without clear labeling. Recorded figures already take precedence — keep that hierarchy visible.
- Cobra’s “about 8” working estimate is appropriately low-confidence and sensitivity-tested. Further promotion on the live site should wait for airframe- or unit-level attribution rather than wider command returns.
- Adding more campaign dossiers or calculators before closing the highest-leverage attribution gaps risks increasing surface area without improving existing user journeys (Normandy, BoB, selected 1944 dates).
- Wikipedia reference sweep and campaign audit added substantial leads; the next value is selective primary follow-up on the gaps above rather than additional tertiary sweeps.

## 4. Recommended next research actions (ranked)

1. **Highest impact for planned milestone and existing calculators**: Obtain and transcribe the Mission 494 / Cobra fighter annex and match German airframe or unit records to the escort encounters. This directly tightens the most visible low-confidence estimate.
2. **Battle of Britain reverse direction**: Locate or compile a consistent German weekly or phase claim series to match the existing RAF side. Enables symmetric validation.
3. **June 1944 type-level audit**: Independently reconcile the provisional Bf 109 / Fw 190 day-by-day candidates against the verified month-end AHB totals before any further use.
4. **Selective primary follow-up**: Prioritize the archival leads already listed in the 12 Nov campaign audit (TsAMO Northwestern/Western files, original AIR 27 for key BoB dates, Luftflotte 3 unit paperwork for Normandy) over new broad searches.
5. **Defer**: Additional campaign overviews or new calculators until at least one of the above attribution gaps is closed or explicitly scoped as research-ledger only.
