# Data extraction from the 24 cited works — 12 November 2026

Systematic extraction of Atlas-relevant quantitative and operational data from the 24 works identified in the Wikipedia reference sweep. This pass covers the accessible titles; inaccessible books and archive files are noted with next steps. Complements earlier partial extractions (Dieppe ORS, Kursk episodes, Bodenplatte formations, 16th Air Army, Tidal Wave planning).

## Extracted data by work

### Accessible and newly extracted

**Nafziger 945GALA (Bodenplatte formations)**  
Secondary compilation citing Girbig. 37 named formations with types and bases (already partially recorded). Full list now confirmed in the PDF: JG 1 (I/II FW 190 A-8 Twenthe/Drope, III Bf 109 G-14 Rheine), JG 2, JG 3, JG 4, JG 6, JG 11, JG 26, JG 27, JG 53, JG 54, JG 77, SG 4, and Me 262 / Ar 234 elements. No claim or loss totals in the formation list itself; these require the Girbig source or Manrho/Pütz ledger.

**Nafziger 945RDAD (16th Air Army, 16 April 1945)**  
Total aircraft 3,188; combat aircraft 3,033. Breakdown: fighters 1,548, ground-attack 687, bombers 684 (533 day + 151 night), reconnaissance 114 (including artillery spotting). Distinct from the previously transcribed headquarters report of 3,037 serviceable aircraft.

**Timokhovich, Soviet Aviation in the Battle of Kursk (1959)**  
Postwar institutional history based on front and air-army documents. Additional quantitative data beyond the eight dated episodes already extracted:
- Defensive battle: average ~1,500 sorties per day; 2nd Air Army alone flew 11,000 sorties 5–14 July, dropping ~1,000 tons of bombs plus large numbers of PTAB, rockets and cannon rounds.
- Claim of >1,500 German aircraft destroyed in the defensive phase; overall battle claim >2,000 German aircraft destroyed in air and on the ground.
- Counteroffensive: >90,000 sorties (approx. 50% ground support, 31% air superiority); >5,000 tons of bombs on the Belgorod–Kharkov axis alone.
These remain Soviet institutional claims and should be kept with that qualification.

**AAFHS-103 (Ploesti / Tidal Wave)**  
Planning figure 154 aircraft and 177 successful takeoffs already recorded. Full scan of the study for target-force tables and loss appendix was not completed in this pass (PDF text extraction returned limited usable content). Loss-by-cause and airframe-level detail still require the mission records.
