# Battle of Britain — milestone 1 start: German claims vs RAF losses (12 November 2026)

First pass on the reverse comparison (German claims against RAF losses) across the 17 weeks and five phases. Builds on the existing Atlas baseline in atlas-v90-campaign-loss-validation-2026-11-12.md and the earlier claims-over-campaign note. Focus is on phase totals and the seven intensive days where both sides have published figures.

## Phase-level comparison (RAF contemporary estimates vs official German records)

Source: Hansard, 14 May 1947 (Battle of Britain Enemy Losses). Figures in brackets are losses admitted in contemporary German communiques.

| Phase | Dates | RAF estimate (destroyed) | German records (destroyed) | German damaged |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Preliminary | 10 Jul–7 Aug | 188 | 192 (63) | 77 |
| Coastal targets | 8–23 Aug | 755 | 430 (213) | 127 |
| Fighter Command airfields | 24 Aug–6 Sep | 643 | 378 (243) | 127 |
| London (heavy bombers) | 7–30 Sep | 846 | 435 (134) | 163 |
| London (fighter-bombers) | 1–31 Oct | 260 | 325 (134) | 163 |
| **Campaign total** | 10 Jul–31 Oct | **2,692** | **1,733 (896)** | **643** |

The two source series that give 1,733 German aircraft destroyed are already noted in the Atlas baseline. The RAF estimate total (2,692) matches the postwar phase comparison already held. The gap between RAF claims and German recorded losses is largest in the August airfield phase and the September London phase.
