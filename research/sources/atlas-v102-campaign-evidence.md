# Atlas v10.2 · Deployed forces, missions and outcomes

All 20 campaigns now have dated mission cases and separate coverage assessments for each side. The Atlas identifies 984 unit memberships across 170 dated lists, up from 297 across 44 in v10.1. The 78 cases link what formations did to what a ship, army, unit or observer recorded afterward. [Campaign reader](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/campaign-evidence.html) · [Roster explorer](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/forces.html)

## Counting the forces

Each membership records a named unit in one dated list. A squadron appearing twice contributes two rows. A headquarters and its subordinate units may both appear, making these counts unsuitable for adding aircraft or estimating total historical force strength. Every campaign has a separate account of effort, losses and effect for each side. The full table also counts known locations, mapped stations, aircraft held, aircraft serviceable and recorded unit activities. ## What changed in the historical record

- At Dieppe all 74 Allied squadron allocations in the appendix have a role and a sourced station. The 48 Spitfire cover allocations include separate Polish and American 308 Squadrons. Two additional coastal search squadrons are documented outside the 74. The German bomber and reconnaissance force still has gaps. [Dieppe reconciliation](v102-dieppe.md)
- At Messina the German ferry diary records that bombing closed the Salvatore jetty and that ferrying continued from another point. No. 92 Squadron’s record identifies its escorts that day. Responsibility for individual ferry damage remains open. [Sicily cases](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/campaign-evidence.html?campaign=sicily)
- At Stalingrad 17 Romanian squadron identities fit the roles and date of the September return of 26. The bomber-group chronology conflicts with other dated deployments. At Brunete the opening Republican fighter total and the identity of the third opposing Fiat squadron remain open at unit level. [Stalingrad](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/campaign-evidence.html?campaign=stalingrad) · [Spain](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/campaign-evidence.html?campaign=spain)
- At Normandy one account gives 21 German reinforcement Gruppen. Eight independently dated movement records identify plausible arrivals. A surviving list matching those names to the 21 has yet to be located. At Arnhem the receiving division supplies a different measure of success: it recovered 21 net tons from the 388 net tons dropped on 19 September. [Normandy](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/campaign-evidence.html?campaign=normandy) · [Market Garden](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/campaign-evidence.html?campaign=marketgarden)
- In the October Schweinfurt report, 29 B-24s were dispatched for the planned strike and 21 took part in the subsequent diversion. The support record identifies the bomber forces escorted by three fighter groups. [Schweinfurt](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/campaign-evidence.html?campaign=schweinfurt)
