# Six under-researched air campaigns: source audit, 6 November 2026

## Decision and accounting boundary

This milestone completes usable *operational histories* for Stalingrad, Reich defence, Kursk, Italy, Bagration and Berlin, the six campaigns with zero campaign-attached historical quantities after the preceding phase-guide work. Figures are evidence observations, **not a homogeneous table of aircraft destroyed**, and must not be summed across campaigns or paired by date without checking population. Original German losses already available in the separate research laboratory are not duplicated as new discoveries; the two selected Reich month totals are attached to their appropriate campaign with their original reporting caveats.

Source classes below mean where a *claim* comes from, not a blanket assertion that every source number is independently transcribed. Maps mark a locality or a campaign-wide schematic location, **never a polygon for all operations**.

## Unresolved, explicitly not populated

1. **Comparable two-sided aircraft combat losses for these six campaigns**: needs matched contemporary formation/sortie returns, date and precise theater. Strength estimates must not be passed off as destroyed aircraft or reconciled enemy claims.
2. **Stalingrad airlift daily cargo ledger**: another secondary history gives **94.16 tons/day** for **25 Nov 1942–2 Feb 1943**, while the chosen secondary reports **117.6 tons/day over seven weeks**. Date windows and calculation bases differ; neither can be transposed to every chapter. Need Luftwaffe transport logs and reported landed vs air-dropped cargo before synthesis.
3. **Bagration strength population**: other historians include or exclude long-range aviation and reserves and give larger estimates. The published 5,300/1,400 comparison is retained as *one source's table*, not a definitive count.
4. **Berlin 16th Air Army 2,738 ambiguity**: see above. This is a material **aircraft-versus-crews** risk and is intentionally excluded from user-facing numeric evidence.
5. **Italian March 1944 bombing denominators**: a different official publication describes a wider **514-aircraft** attack interval/participation measure. The sourced 435 is limited to 08:30–12:00 over Cassino area; a flight-by-flight original air force mission return would be required to compare them. The 300 tons is approximate and town-local, not a campaign munitions total.
6. **German Reich cause classifications**: existing March April scan figures are original German accounting, including non-combat write-offs. They overlap with the Reich loss chart in the separately opened research laboratory and must **not** be added to that chart as independent German losses.

## Navigation semantics

- Stages lead from the overall historical question to air operations and *then* to a dated inspectable incident, not from campaign title directly to an arbitrary weekday.
- A phase is an editorial explanatory interval, not an independent statistic. A single documented event may illustrate only part of the phase. Phase limits say so.
- The two actual single-day operations Dieppe and Bodenplatte stay unphased by design.
- No special missing/zero substitution was introduced. The published full-war USAAF, RAF, VVS and German lab datasets retain their original populations and filters.
