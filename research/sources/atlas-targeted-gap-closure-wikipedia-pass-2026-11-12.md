## Battle of Britain — German claims after 15 August
- AHB translations of Luftwaffe Führungsstab IC situation reports stop at Part 3 (1–15 August). No further AHB parts for late August–October are listed on the RAF site.
- Secondary sources (Air Power Review, battle-of-britain-diary.org.uk) confirm the known overclaim pattern: e.g. 24 Aug 57 claimed / 20 actual; 15 Sep 78 claimed / 29 actual.
- Campaign-scale German claim total remains ~3,058. No consistent weekly series after mid-August is available from the AHB translations.

## Eastern Front — additional strength anchors
- Barbarossa opening: ~20 fighter Gruppen (~1,000 single-engine fighters). JG 51 claimed ~500 Soviet aircraft by mid-July for the loss of 3 Bf 109s.
- Mid-1942: ~4,000+ combat aircraft on the Eastern Front; ~550 Bf 109s total.
- Kursk (5 July 1943): Luftwaffe ~1,787 aircraft (354 fighters) vs Soviet ~3,028 (1,414 fighters). First-day German losses later tallied at 55 aircraft / 20 pilots (fighter force 23 / 12). Campaign claims 1,735 for 64 lost over the full operation.
- January 1944: ~1,700 of ~5,500 front-line combat aircraft on the Eastern Front. By Bagration (June 1944) only ~300 serviceable fighters across the entire front.

## Cobra / Ninth Air Force July 1944
- Eighth Air Force Mission 494 (25 July): 1,581 bombers + 500 fighters; 5 bombers + 2 fighters lost. Short-bombing killed 102 US troops (including Lt Gen McNair) and wounded 380.
- Ninth Air Force daily sorties and losses appear in secondary compilations (e.g. 6 July: 509 bomb sorties / 2 lost / 110 damaged; 1,159 fighter-bomber sorties / 7 lost). Full July command returns and the Ninth Air Force July report are still not online.
- Mission 494 tactical report exists at CARL (cgsc.contentdm.oclc.org) but is not freely downloadable in full text here.
