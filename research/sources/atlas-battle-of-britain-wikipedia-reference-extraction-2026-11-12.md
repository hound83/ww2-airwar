# Battle of Britain — additional figures from Wikipedia-linked sources (12 November 2026)

Extraction of quantitative data from Wikipedia pages and the secondary sources they cite, focused on German claims, losses and related figures that can supplement the existing AHB translation and Churchill series notes.

## Key additional figures

### Adlertag (13 August)
- RAF Fighter Command claimed 78 German aircraft (another source gives official RAF claims as 64).
- Actual German losses: 47–48 destroyed, 39 severely damaged.
- Luftwaffe claimed 70 Hurricanes and Spitfires in the air + 18 Blenheims in the air + 84 RAF fighters on the ground.
- Actual RAF losses: 13 fighters and 11 bombers in the air, 47 aircraft of various kinds on the ground.
Source: Wikipedia Adlertag page, citing secondary accounts.

### 15 September (Battle of Britain Day)
- Wikipedia Battle of Britain Day page: RAF 29 aircraft destroyed / ~21 damaged; German 58 destroyed / 20 severely damaged.
- Secondary citations note the British claimed 81, with over-claiming visible (e.g. one Dornier the subject of nine claims).
- One secondary source gives 60 German and 26 RAF aircraft shot down.

### Kanalkampf (Channel Battle) losses
Wikipedia Kanalkampf page (mobile summary):
- RAF: 115 fighters destroyed, 42 damaged, 71 pilots killed, 19 wounded, 4 missing.
- Luftwaffe: 80 fighters destroyed, 36 damaged; 22 dive bombers destroyed, 22 damaged; 100 medium bombers destroyed, 33 damaged; 13 naval aircraft destroyed, 1 damaged; 201 airmen killed, 75 wounded, 277 missing, 16 PoW.

### German campaign-scale losses (German Wikipedia)
Luftschlacht um England:
- 1 July–31 October 1940: 1,791 German aircraft destroyed in combat (of which 1,386 by enemy action).
- 2,000 Luftwaffe personnel killed, 2,600 missing or captured.
- RAF: 1,693 aircraft destroyed in combat (Fighter, Bomber, Coastal Command); 544 Fighter Command pilots killed.

### Strength and pilot figures (Adlertag / secondary)
- 17 August: Luftwaffe 2,226 operational aircraft.
- By 14 September: Bf 109 units at 67% establishment, Bf 110 at 46%, bombers at 59%; one week later 64%, 52%, 52%.
- RAF pilot losses cited as 125 per week in the late-August peak; short by 150 pilots by 31 August.
- From 24 August to 6 September: 295 RAF fighters totally destroyed and 171 badly damaged (against output of 269 new/repaired Spitfires and Hurricanes).

### Other cited daily claims
- 15 August: German high command claimed 99 RAF fighters destroyed in the air (RAF actual 34; German losses ~75).
- 13 August: German claim of 132 British aircraft destroyed (secondary citations, already noted).

## Status
These figures are secondary or tertiary citations from Wikipedia pages. They provide useful cross-checks and additional daily/phase snapshots but do not replace the primary AHB situation-report extracts already logged. No new consistent weekly German claim series covering late August–October was located in the Wikipedia references examined.

## Atlas implication
The Adlertag and 15 September claim/loss pairs, the Kanalkampf loss totals, and the German Wikipedia campaign figures (1,791 destroyed) are ready for review against the existing Churchill weekly series and Hansard phase totals. The principal open item remains a full weekly German claim series after mid-August.
