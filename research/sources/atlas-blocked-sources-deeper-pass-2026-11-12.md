# Deeper pass on catalogue-only / blocked sources — 12 November 2026

Follow-up extraction attempt on the remaining titles from the 24-work Wikipedia sweep (Frappé, Manrho & Pütz, Davis workbooks, Caldwell & Muller, Stilla, CAMO, AIR 24, Bundesarchiv, etc.). Results of what is actually reachable are logged below. No live calculator changes.

## Source-by-source results

### Jean-Bernard Frappé, *La Luftwaffe face au débarquement*
- No full text, PDF, or archive.org scan located.
- Catalogue record and secondary reviews confirm day-by-day Jagdgeschwader treatment with claims/loss annexes, but 25 July entries remain untranscribed.
- Status: still blocked for quantitative extraction.

### Manrho & Pütz, *Bodenplatte: The Luftwaffe’s Last Hope*
- Full book on archive.org but access-restricted (borrow/print-disabled).
- Wikipedia and secondary citations give high-level totals (Luftwaffe ~280 aircraft lost, 234 pilots killed/missing/captured; Allied ~305 destroyed + 190 damaged on the ground), but the aircraft-level ledger is not extractable without the restricted scan.
- Status: high-level totals only; detailed loss register still blocked.

### Davis, *Bombing the European Axis Powers* workbooks
- Sheet key PDF successfully retrieved: https://www.airuniversity.af.edu/Portals/10/AUPress/Books/Davis_Disk/Worksheets/1_Sheet_Key.pdf
- Key defines columns (countries, air-force codes, target struck categories, sighting methods, Atk/Lost/tonnage fields, area codes).
- Individual year workbooks (1939-41.xls, 1942.xls, 1943.xls, 1944-1.xls, etc.) are listed on the Air University site but returned access errors in this pass. Forum mirrors exist but were not bulk-downloaded.
- Status: sheet key now available; full mission-level rows still require workbook recovery.

### Caldwell & Muller, *The Luftwaffe Over Germany*
- Full book on archive.org but access-restricted.
- Related Muller article (Air & Space Power Journal, 2003) accessible and supplies selected figures: by July 1944 fighter-pilot trainees received <25 hours in operational types vs. >150 for US pilots; 1944 AAF fighter losses to flak exceeded those to fighters; final-year day-fighter claims of 703 AAF heavies vs. 1,579 in the preceding year.
- Status: selected secondary figures extracted; full book tables still blocked.

### Stilla, *Die Luftwaffe im Kampf um die Luftherrschaft*
- PDF previously located on lexikon-der-wehrmacht.de; numerical tables not newly extracted in this pass.
- Status: tables still open.

### Archive files (CAMO OKL Findbuch 12452, AIR 24, Bundesarchiv RL 35, etc.)
- CAMO OKL catalogue page accessible: 678 files, metadata for many 1939–1945 items visible, including some 1944 operational reports (e.g., Akte 209 American daytime raids 11 April 1944, Akte 216 May 1944 operations, Akte 219 Normandy invasion air operations).
- Individual file pages require user-agreement acceptance; no bulk loss-return transcription completed.
- AIR 24 / 2nd TAF ORB and Bundesarchiv RL 35 remain catalogue-level only.
- Status: catalogue and selected file titles confirmed; document-level transcription still required.
