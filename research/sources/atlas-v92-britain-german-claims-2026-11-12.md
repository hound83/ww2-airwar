# German claims and British aircraft losses in the Battle of Britain

The reverse comparison covers the existing 17 reporting weeks and five phases from 10 July to 31 October 1940. It adds 114 daily British airframe-loss observations, 34 daily German staff fighter-claim subtotals, three explicit early report gaps and seven raid or unit checks. The previous seven German-to-RAF comparisons from Cox and the campaign total quoted by Richards retain their own scopes. [Live comparison by week](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/grand-air-war-v80.html?loss-scope=britain-campaign&loss-series=britain-german-weeks#claim-loss-calculator) · [Machine-readable dates and sources](../transcriptions/battle-of-britain-campaign-claims-1940.json)

## Source definitions

- **British airframe losses:** Fagan's appendix B, table B.1, reproduces a daily reconstruction from Ramsey. The 114 days sum to 1,023. The Atlas applies the existing Hansard phase boundaries to those dates. It retains Churchill's 915 fighters written off or missing as its own published weekly series. The totals have different reporting bases. On 15 September the Fagan table has 31 and Cox's comparison has 29. The available tables do not reconcile the extra two by serial or cause. [Fagan, table B.1, PDF pp. 153–156 and ch. 2 n. 16](https://etheses.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/28781/1/Fagan_203040680_CorrectedThesisClean.pdf#page=153) · [Churchill, Aircraft Losses table](https://www.fadedpage.com/books/20191054/html.php) · [Cox, claim table and n. 49](https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/centre-for-air-and-space-power-studies/aspr/apr-vol3-iss3-3-pdf/)
- **German fighter claims:** Sum the named Spitfires, Hurricanes, Defiants, Curtiss aircraft and explicit fighter identifications in the air-combat part of each operation day's enemy-loss summary. The data preserve the main daily report scope and page. Ground and AA claims, probables, balloons and separately dated late entries stay outside the subtotal. An explicit nil return or a readable summary without a fighter claim in air combat gives zero for this measure. An unresolved summary gives a gap. The returns are German staff assessments, with some target identifications explicitly tentative. Individual pilot submissions and final credited victories would require their own sources. [AHB translation 119](https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/our-history/air-historical-branch/ahb-translations-from-captured-german-documents/situation-reports-issued-by-luftwaffe-fuehrungsstab-ic-part-1-30-june-13-july-1940/) · [AHB translation 122](https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/our-history/air-historical-branch/ahb-translations-from-captured-german-documents/situation-reports-issued-by-luftwaffe-fuehrungsstab-ic-part-2-14-july-31-july-1940/) · [AHB translation 123](https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/our-history/air-historical-branch/ahb-translations-from-captured-german-documents/situation-reports-issued-by-luftwaffe-fuehrungsstab-ic-part-3-1-august-15-august-1940/)
- **Additional staff survey:** The 1 July–15 August table reports 558 fighter aircraft claimed by German fighters, 574 fighter aircraft across the listed causes and 823 aircraft of all types. Its reporting interval includes 1–9 July and its methodology differs from the daily operation summaries. Keep it separate from the 523 fighter claims in the 34 transcribed daily subtotals and from Richards's 3,058 claims for the whole battle. [AHB translation 123, PDF p. 122](https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/our-history/air-historical-branch/ahb-translations-from-captured-german-documents/situation-reports-issued-by-luftwaffe-fuehrungsstab-ic-part-3-1-august-15-august-1940/#page=122) · [Richards, ch. VI, p. 190 n. 4](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/UK-RAF-I/UK-RAF-I-6.html)

The initial 10–13 July reporting window and 28 July–3 August week have a figure for every day. Four other weeks have partial coverage and eleven have no count under this definition. The seven later Cox dates appear separately and are flagged in their periods. The early gaps are 17 July (ambiguous late Spitfire), 23 July (cause breakdown unavailable in the summary) and 4 August (enemy losses unknown). A late Hurricane for 20 July and a late Spitfire for 8 August remain linked in the notes. Source populations and cause coverage prevent a like-for-like campaign estimator from these series.

## Review of existing work and archive leads

Grok's milestone notes identify the Churchill series, the 1 July–15 August staff survey and the three RAF translations. Churchill's columns already match the Atlas 17 weeks exactly. The German column measures German own losses. The Hansard online phase transcription has the known 430/403 discrepancy, and the Overy sample runs from May into November, outside the Atlas period. The baseline keeps the previous reconciled phase figures and weekly tally. The German survey's 670 by fighters includes bombers and other targets. Its fighter-type subset in that column is 558. [Existing campaign reconciliation](atlas-battle-of-britain-claims-over-campaign-2026-11-11.md) · [Grok's investigation](atlas-battle-of-britain-german-claims-investigation-2026-11-12.md)

The TsAMO catalogue identifies overlapping July and August material and later returns in files 98–101, with a catalogue gap on 30 September. The underlying scans require acceptance of the archive's terms. This pass verified the catalogue dates. Cox cites AHB translation R.353 for later dates, and the RAF archive uses AIR 27 for squadron ORBs and AIR 50 for individual combat reports. A pilot-list compilation contains duplicate entries and inconsistent coverage labels, so it remains a locator for individual cases.
