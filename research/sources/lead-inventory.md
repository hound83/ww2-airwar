## Source triage guidance

- When encountering a promising book, photograph, national archive or digitized
  unit summary, retain citation, link, call number, scan page, original language,
  reporting force, period and potential future visual use even if currently out
  of scope. Avoid discarding leads for lack of immediate chart placement.
- Check copyright/permissions before redistributing source *documents*. The
  research catalog records bibliographic facts and short descriptions, not
  unlawfully copied books or whole copyrighted datasets.
- Verify literal archive values against internally summed controls where possible.
  A metadata or transcription discrepancy is a lead to investigate, not a license
  to invent a convenient number.
