# Opposing-force aircraft-loss comparison audit: all campaigns and dated events
Date: 10 November 2026. Scope: 20 European Air War Atlas campaign dossiers and 83 detailed dated events.

## Method and classification
- Every campaign has a visible **opposing-loss evidence coverage** summary; every dated event shows a separate exact-date evidence panel.
- **Paired own-loss observations** require dated records of aircraft lost by both sides in the same event. Even here the national accounting may differ by type, loss threshold, geography, or military-service population; *never automatically compute an exchange ratio*.
- **Claim versus recorded loss** is explicitly distinct from two parties' own losses: a claimed victory is not a confirmed enemy aircraft loss nor the claimant's own loss.
- **Partial day evidence** is displayed with the exact source and not auto-assigned to the opponent.
- **No matched event data** is stated rather than substituting campaign totals or zeros.
- Event-date matching is STRICT: only facts dated exactly YYYY-MM-DD matching the chosen event; broad monthly/period estimates are not misassigned to one day. For existing facts where side is not independently typed, the interface labels the side as unclassified, rather than guessing.
- Campaign-level comparison panels already available for 1940 Britain, Barbarossa, Sicily, and Normandy are retained; Britain adds a phase/day source table and all other campaigns now have status coverage.

## Dieppe, 19 August 1942: genuinely same-day national-loss observations
Canadian Army **official** postwar history based on captured original German documents:
https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/Canada/CA/OpSumm/OpSumm-5.html
reports 106 RAF aircraft lost, 48 German aircraft **destroyed** and 24 German aircraft **damaged**. The two German categories must not be conflated as '72 destroyed'. The Royal Canadian Air Force separately cautions the accepted totals are debated:
https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/maple-leaf/rcaf/2019/08/in-the-skies-above-dieppe-august-19-1942.html

## Schweinfurt 14 October 1943: own losses vs German actual and US gunners' claims
National WWII Museum (secondary museum reconstruction based on German loss documents):
https://www.nationalww2museum.org/war/articles/black-thursday-october-14-1943-second-schweinfurt-bombing-raid
reports German fighters **31 destroyed**, **12 written off**, **34 damaged**, alongside USAAF bomber gunners' **186 German aircraft claims**. These are distinct airframe-loss/damage/claim categories. The actual USAAF own loss count of **60 B-17s shot down** is already sourced in Atlas to National Museum USAF (https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/1519661/black-thursday-schweinfurt-october-14-1943/). The alternative museum reading 251 'on the mission' versus 291 dispatched is preserved, and neither denominator is automatically used for the German losses.

## Existing bilateral coverage retained
- **Bodenplatte 1 Jan 1945:** 224 Allied aircraft destroyed and around 300 German lost on the day; the German figure is approximate and the local Eindhoven 45 is a geographic subset of Allied total. Do not add damage and destruction.
- **Britain August serviceability:** different Fighter Command vs German sampled dates and unit scope.
- **Normandy:** Allied D-Day own losses versus regional German losses from a month-long window; *not* a valid exchange ratio.
- **Barbarossa:** German own recorded losses versus German claims of Soviet losses, not verified VVS reciprocal records.
- **Sicily:** recorded Germany/Italy daytime and night destruction first week versus Allied full-campaign HQ MAAF own losses, different windows.
- **Bagration:** Soviet 16th Army daily own lost planes and its unverified German kills; original German Quartermaster reciprocal losses still missing.

## Research expansion and unresolved requirements
The user requested evidence for ALL 20 campaigns and all 83 events. This milestone creates a source-aware discovery/status at every user entry point, **not a claim that all 83 now have a genuine two-sided loss ledger**. Many lack original opposing-day reports.
Next archival priority: original German Gen.Qu. 6.Abt loss rolls 1939–45 and RAF AHB German captured-table translations, TsAMO Soviet VVS squadron/unit daily returns, original Italian Regia Aeronautica logs, RAF AIR 27 and USAAF MACRs; transcribe losses with archival IDs, date, geographic boundaries and destroyed-versus-damaged thresholds, then reconcile to existing claims. All would remain absent rather than inferred until verified.

Do not automatically derive a historical kill ratio from damaged versus destroyed, claims versus own losses, fleet strength versus serviceability, or disjoint date ranges.
