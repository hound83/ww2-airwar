## Notes
- The Luftwaffe column is recorded German losses (destroyed), not German claims. It aligns with the 1,733 total from official German records in the Hansard phase table.
- Peak German losses fall in the week to 17 August (261) and the week to 31 August (193), corresponding to the intensive airfield and coastal phases.
- The series is weekly (not strictly calendar-week aligned in every row) and therefore needs mapping onto the Atlas 17-week structure before use in a phase or week model.
- German claims against RAF aircraft remain a separate series; the accessible German intelligence survey for 1 July–15 August (Luftwaffe situation report 345) claims 823 RAF aircraft destroyed in that partial window, but a full campaign German claim series is still open.

## Atlas implication
This is the strongest open weekly German loss series for the reverse comparison. It can be reviewed against the existing Atlas 17-week RAF loss baseline and the Hansard phase totals. A full German claim series (as opposed to German losses) is still missing.
