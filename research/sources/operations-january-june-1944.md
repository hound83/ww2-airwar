# January–June 1944 air operations and evidence coverage

The [mission timeline](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/operations-1944.html) and [source-linked JSON](../transcriptions/operations-january-june-1944.json) cover operations recorded from 1 January through 30 June 1944. Each entry identifies its date, reporting force, evidence type, original source or transcription, and a locator. The filtered CSV carries the same numeric fields, qualifications, source links and evidence connections.

## Dataset coverage

| Reporting population | Dated records | Method and limits |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| RAF Bomber Command | 1,246 | Air Historical Branch volumes V and VI, Appendix 10. Raid and support entries preserve the dispatch, attack and missing columns where legible. Overnight entries retain both dates. |
| US Eighth Air Force | 261 | Numbered chronology entries and selected supporting reports. Separate waves retain a shared mission number. Escort can include Ninth Air Force aircraft. |
| US Fifteenth Air Force | 125 | Dated association target register, chronology and formation reports. Individual totals can apply to one listed attack within a command day. |
| Luftflotte Reich | 74 | 73 daily aircraft-loss rows from RAF AHB VII/138 and one separately marked Allied intelligence estimate for May. |
| Luftflotte 3 and ZG 26 | 13 | Western Front reports, historical studies, estimates and ZG 26 diary evidence. A further 12 command-month returns cover the Reich and Western Front, separately from the daily ledger. |

These 1,719 ledger records describe different reporting levels and activities. A formation subset can overlap its command total. The source audit and data preserve those scopes for comparison.

## Big Week validation

- The Eighth's Mission 226 dispatched **1,003** bombers across three forces. The target listings account for **880** attacking. The chronology records **21** bomber losses and **835** escort aircraft for forces 1 and 3, including Ninth Air Force participation. [Carter and Mueller, 20 February, Mission 226](https://aircrewremembered.com/USAAFCombatOperations/Feb.44.html)
- The February online chronology repeats Missions 237–239 under 24 and 28 February. The ledger places them on 28 February and recovers Missions 233–234 on 24 February using the unit history and official campaign account. For Mission 233, the reported 304 + 266 + 239 dispatches give **809** aircraft. The Rostock, Schweinfurt and Gotha force losses sum to **49**. Gotha and Eisenach attacking counts need further reconciliation. [445th Bomb Group, Mission 28](https://445bg.org/mission-28.html) · [Craven and Cate, vol. III, chapter 2, pp. 38–39](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/AAF/III/AAF-III-2.html)
- The five Eighth bombing mission loss figures in this ledger for 20, 21, 22, 24 and 25 February sum to **158**. The official campaign history and Murray give **137** for the six days, and USAAF Historical Study No. 122 gives **156**. Different reported losses and force boundaries remain unresolved. The Fifteenth's 25 February Regensburg count differs across all three studies. The ledger retains the original scopes and references. [Craven and Cate](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/AAF/III/AAF-III-2.html) · [Murray, chapter 6](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/AAF/AAF-Luftwaffe/AAF-Luftwaffe-6.html) · [USAAF Historical Study 122, pp. 109–111](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/AAF/AAFHS/AAFHS-122.pdf)
- For the RAF Leipzig attack of **19/20 February**, Appendix 10 gives **823 dispatched**, **650 attacking** and **78 missing**. Stuttgart, Schweinfurt and Augsburg retain separate night entries. [RAF AHB, vol. V, Appendix 10](https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/our-history/air-historical-branch/second-world-war-campaign-narratives/raf-in-the-bombing-offensive-against-germany-vol-v-the-full-offensive-february-1943-to-february-1944/)
- The six Luftflotte Reich daily returns for 20–25 February sum to **260 aircraft destroyed by enemy action and 71 damaged by enemy action** across all aircraft types in the command area. The command records include missing aircraft inside enemy-action destruction and also report operational and non-operational losses in separate columns. Treat the sum as a date- and command-scoped figure. [RAF AHB VII/138, each day's all-types total](https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/our-history/air-historical-branch/ahb-translations-from-captured-german-documents/luftwaffe-losses-in-the-area-of-luftflotte-reich-january-april-1944/)
- The III./ZG 26 diary documents a B-24 engagement near Holzminden on 24 February. Date, area and aircraft type support a possible link to the Gotha force in Mission 233. The specific American unit and time alignment are unresolved. The Allied Schweinfurt day/night sequence and the coordinated Regensburg attacks have direct evidence in the official campaign history. [Kogler diary, 24 February](https://aviationtrails.co.uk/luftwaffe-pages/luftwaffe-diaries-big-week/) · [Craven and Cate, pp. 38–41](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/AAF/III/AAF-III-2.html)

## Transcription and reconciliation

- The RAF diaries contain both individual and stacked entries. Numeric fields were kept when their indexed cells aligned with the target or purpose. Implausible attacking counts, ambiguous cells and unknown figures are blank. One unresolved example is the 1/2 January Berlin entry, whose indexed attacking count exceeds its dispatch count. Numeric qualifiers such as approximately and at least remain explicit in the dataset and downloads.
- US mission records list locations named in the operation entry. They can include planned, actual, alternate and diversion targets. A dispatch or loss subtotal applies only to the force described in the record. Claims reflect the original reporting population and classification.
- Luftflotte Reich daily loss returns use the all-types row with six cause and outcome columns. Clearly aligned blank cells in those totals represent an empty category. For January–April, the daily sums are compared with the corresponding official command totals. West and May/June scan layouts require more image verification for comparable daily returns. Reported June operations and Allied intelligence estimates are labelled by source.
- The RAF monthly figures are independent all-operations returns from volume V Appendix 20 and volume VI Appendix 12. The ledger assigns each overnight record to its start month. Diary capture and the published monthly totals differ in all six months, including a February overage. The comparison exposes the difference while the dates and transcription are checked further.
- The Eighth's numbered bomber mission days are compared with published days of operation from Historical Study No. 122, Table 14. The Fifteenth's dated coverage uses the target register and chronology. A compatible monthly command-day denominator remains open for the Fifteenth.
- The Davis January–June workbook remains an access gap. The Air University download returned 403, and the alternate hostname and archive index were unavailable through the retrieval route used. The indexed RAF Night Raid Reports remain a source for resolving Appendix 10 ambiguities. [Davis supplemental workbooks](https://www.airuniversity.af.edu/AUPress/Articles/Article/2181692/online-copies-of-supplemental-cd-rom-files-from-bombing-the-european-axis-powers/) · [Bomber Command Night Raid Reports](https://bombercommandmuseumarchives.ca/menu_sub_bombercommand_nightraidreports.html)
