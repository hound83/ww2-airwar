# Grand Air War: closing the three 1988 USAAF field-chronology research cases

**31 October 2026 — research only, PR #5.** Case data: [typed evidence ledger](../transcriptions/usaaf-1944-1945-three-preoperational-airfield-case-resolutions.json). Integrity test: [targeted provenance/map-gating assertions](../../tests/usaaf-three-airfield-chronology-review.cjs).

The three questions arose in the 75-row OCR priority review. **They were not three verified aircraft deployments.** Subsequent searches clarified two important category errors: (a) a *unit, reconnaissance party or flight* can be in a locality before an all-weather runway becomes operational, and (b) a *unit's regional location* is not necessarily the airfield identified with the region in a later code index.

## Evidentiary status

**Finding:** The *reason for* each apparent contradiction is now historically intelligible, but the exact pre-operational airstrip identity / day-based engineering occupancy is **not proved** for any of the three. No original 1944–45 unit operations diary, engineer daily work report or wartime plot plan was acquired and read in this focused web search. Official USAF lineage books are postwar retrospective compilations, not contemporary diaries.

The original 75-row [audit](../transcriptions/usaaf-1988-priority-75-source-text-review-1944-1945.json) retains its historical flags for provenance. This additional three-case file is a **superseding interpretive overlay**, keyed by `sourceRowId`. Neither file is wired to live site data. No verified exact historical pins are added.

**Practical dashboard principle:** portray *unit presence at city/region*, *airfield opening*, and *verified operating airfield assignment* as different evidence types. Do not collapse them into a single pin or assume date match proves the exact runway.
