# Original unit returns and campaign reporting: source audit (6 November 2026)

## Research approach
Priority was original day-by-day RAF squadron Operations Record Books and historical command returns, with a separate contemporaneous Allied headquarters statistic source to connect operational tempo to losses and claims. No absent daily returns were filled by extrapolation.

### Norway, 28 May and 2 June 1940
**Official archival source:** RAF Air Historical Branch, *The Campaign in Norway, April–June 1940*, sections concerning operations over Narvik, especially printed pp. 89 and 97–99. https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/our-history/air-historical-branch/second-world-war-campaign-narratives/campaign-in-norway-april-june-1940/

- **28 May:** 42 operational flights by No. 263 Squadron (Gladiators); 53 by No. 46 Squadron (Hurricanes). The official narrative explicitly identifies these as separate squadron counts for that day, drawn from contemporary ORBs.
- **2 June:** 75 operational flights and 24 combats by Nos. 263 and 46 **jointly**. The available summary does *not* disaggregate that day's sortie totals between the two squadrons. Combats are not necessarily victories or aircraft destroyed.
- **Northern Norway period total:** 329 flights for No. 263 Squadron and 249 flights for No. 46 Squadron, each across twelve operational days during the May–June deployment. These are different period totals from the **49 flights** at Lesjaskog in the initial April deployment. The 42 and 53 figures on May 28 are **subsets**, never values to add to the 329 and 249 totals.
- The official author cautions that pilots' German-aircraft victory **claims** are not independently audited German losses, and records that lack of enemy detailed return prevents certainty on opponent losses.

**Underlying primary unit archive:** National Archives AIR 27, Forms 540 (daily summary) and 541 (sorties/operational detail). General guide: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/royal-air-force-operations-record-books-1911-1963/ . No. 263 Squadron original AIR 27/1547 transcription scans: https://263squadron.weebly.com/orb.html . The *direct* day-level 28 May / 2 June numbers above were independently found in the official RAF historical campaign narrative that cites the original ORBs; they were **not** re-transcribed aircraft-by-aircraft from every Form 541 image. No. 46 Squadron Form 541 has not yet been independently transcribed.

### Sicily, 10 July–17 August 1943
**Original wartime command publication:** Headquarters Mediterranean Allied Air Forces, *Air Power in the Mediterranean: November 1942–February 1945*, prepared 18 February and circulated 27 February 1945 under Lt. Gen. Ira C. Eaker, printed p. 13, *Major Campaigns*. Original scanned headquarters compilation: https://znaci.org/00002/315.pdf .

The **Sicily** reporting row is explicitly scoped to **10 July–17 August 1943**:
- **25,070** operational sorties (flown missions, NOT 25,070 aircraft).
- **16,243** tons of bombs (a different physical measure).
- **293** Allied claims of enemy aircraft (NOT independently confirmed Axis losses).
- **177** Allied aircraft losses (NOT the same as Allied claims and not an opposing-loss record).

These four quantities form one reporting population in the original command table; they must not be conflated with Western Naval Task Force's separately collected **~440 fighter-cover sorties per day** at three western landing beaches, or **~200 enemy flight tracks** recorded across a shorter reporting window. Source: https://www.history.navy.mil/research/library/online-reading-room/title-list-alphabetically/s/the-sicilian-campaign-operation-husky.html .

### Other original documents located but not promoted to numeric data
- RAF AHB: *Report by Northwest African Tactical Air Force on Operations in the Capture of Sicily*, available from https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/our-history/air-historical-branch/despatches-and-reports/ . Requires independent table transcription and unit/sortie-population audit before ingestion.
- RAF AHB: *Mare Nostrum*, contemporary MACAF coastal effort; reports weekly invasion-period convoy escort flying, but not identical in window or population to the Sicily row. https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/our-history/air-historical-branch/ahb-despatches-and-reports/mare-nostrum-a-review-of-macaf-operations-march1943-september1944/ .
- U.S. Army Air Forces dated mission chronology, 10–11 July 1943, locates targets but does not itself support the new HQ MAAF 177-loss series: https://www.armyaircorpsmuseum.org/july-1943-army-air-forces.cfm .
- Berlin, Barbarossa: no newly verified *original unit day-level* combat-loss registers were recovered in this session; pre-existing sourced numbers are left untouched.

## Integrity boundary
Original report / contemporary statistical summary / retrospective official narrative / individual unit ORB are not equivalent; the Atlas displays their distinctions. This is source recovery, not independent archival verification of all underlying flight logs. Missing data remain missing and no cross-side casualty ratio is computed.
