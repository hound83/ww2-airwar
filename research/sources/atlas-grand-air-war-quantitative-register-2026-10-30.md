# Atlas: Grand Air War quantitative research register

Research began 30 October 2026. Scope: **Europe, 1936–May 1945**, all belligerent air services and air branches, not just fighter aircraft. Covers aircraft strength, serviceability, unit order of battle and location, sortie output, combat claims, aircraft losses by cause/type, and aircrew/personnel casualties. **This is a research register, not an assertion that the live Atlas has these data.**

## Known contradictions and semantic traps

1. **Soviet 1944 aircraft-loss populations:** annual VVS KA combat-by-type series in [GA-01 Table 06-21](https://www.rkka.es/Estadisticas/VVS_stat/06/06_21.htm) reports **7,861**; [separate 1944 writeoff table](https://www.rkka.es/Estadisticas/VVS_stat/06/agregado_otros_documentos/bajas_totales_1944.htm) reports **8,036** combat/missing written off, **3,750** accident and **7,593** wear/obsolete (19,379 overall). **175 difference in 1944 combat**, and these are **not additive independent loss populations**. Audit original footnotes, scope, cutoff and type before selecting.
2. **USAAF all-theater dead count:** Statistical Digest Table 34 says **40,061** died, whereas [official Army Air Forces history Vol. VII ch. 13](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/AAF/VII/AAF-VII-13.html) is reported as **40,451** killed. Could be cutoff/definition/revision; no automatic correction. ETO **Table 36** all personnel **63,410 battle casualties** (19,876 died, 8,413 wounded/evacuated, 35,121 missing/interned/captured) and MTO **Table 37** 31,155 casualties are **not pilot fatality totals**.
3. **Soviet pilot deaths vs personnel deaths:** Krivosheev-derived tables have dissimilar definitions (pilots vs wider staff; casualties vs killed/missing; calendar and cohort). Preserve role and fate codes, don't add dissimilar counts.
4. **Claims are a *different object* from loss:** British AIR 50, Polish Bajana, Romanian government claimed victories, Soviet pilot ace lists and German claims may refer to the same aircraft, shared credits, or exaggerated observations. Maintain `claim_id` linked to potential loss incident, but never make `claim_total === enemy_loss_total`.
5. **Finnish unit casualties:** Official individual-record code `S` means death not caused by enemy action. Our **477** squadron-linked deaths include **210 `S`**, and the source has no direct pilot/aircrew role in the extract; presenting all 477 as fighter-pilot KIA would be seriously wrong. Match by precise `LLv`/`Le.Lv`, not generic text containing `Ilm` (e.g. **P/L Ilmarinen** was a ship).
6. **Timing and front:** Luftwaffe reported-date vs event-date, delayed corrections; Soviet annual air army migrating between fronts; Romania switching sides 23Aug1944; Greece/Poland/Free France/Commonwealth under RAF affiliation. Each unit needs a **dated parent command and theater**, otherwise aggregation can double-count or misplace sorties on the map.
7. **Publication rights:** numeric historical facts can be independently researched and cited. Government open data such as Finnish CC BY4 and Dutch RCE CC0 can be directly processed with attribution; other specialist commercial datasets, scans, images and full original editorial databases require rights-by-source examination. The catalogue is a lead, not a bundled clone.

## What the Atlas needs before claiming comprehensive coverage

The current displayed dataset is strong on a few fighter/Western Front checkpoints, but weak on **bombers, ground attack, reconnaissance, transport, naval/coastal, Mediterranean, East, Spanish prelude and smaller air forces**. Build a normalized ledger with these minimal record types (all allow **unknown/missing** values):

1. `unit`, `unit_alias`, `unit_parent_at_date`, `belligerent_alignment_at_date`; never identify only from a short numeric code.
2. `strength_return`: date, unit/theater, role/type/variant, establishment, on-hand, serviceable, operational crews, provenance.
3. `operation`: event date/time, unit, actual sorties versus missions, mission class, origin/target geography, source, confidence; counters for day/night.
4. `aircraft_incident`: type/serial, event/report dates, unit, damage/writeoff, combat/ground/accident/wear, crew fate, corroborating archival IDs.
5. `personnel_casualty`: unit, role (pilot, gunner, navigator, ground crew, unknown), date, died/wounded/missing/POW/returned/unknown, linked aircraft when known.
6. `claim`: reporting unit/person, claimed enemy type, claim date/location, destroyed/probable/damaged, original status, optional independently matched actual incident.
7. `source_observation`: original text/page/folio, version, compiler, rights, exact force+period+geographic **denominator**, recorded/compiled/estimated/modelled/missing confidence.

**Coverage discipline:** a date or unit is displayed only when actual observations exist. Unverified population totals must stay in research rather than live dashboards; unanswered weeks/months must stay blank. Use source-linked crosswalks to roll unit → group/air army → theater → force; de-duplicate before showing nation totals; make both `geographic theater` and `national/service` selectable without counting Commonwealth service twice.

## Recommended extraction sequence (research, not UI implementation)

1. **Immediate quantitative bulk with clean provenance:** finish RKKA.es Part 2 and 6 (air armies 1941–45, sortie mission roles, units & readiness, ground/aircraft losses, PVO/VMF/ADD), digitize USAAF Statistical Digest Tables 24/36/37/84/119/120, and review already imported rows first. Validate RKKA against Soviet 1962 original. Material already freely online.
2. **Deep pilot-scale unit test:** one complete campaign/month in RAF AIR 27/50, German Generalquartiermeister and Eighth AF mission/MACR records, with claims-versus-reported-loss reconciliation. Use geographic units and pilots only when verified. This establishes the import schema before a nationwide event flood.
3. **Broaden both axes:** Italian 1940–43 / post-Armistice, French 1940, Spain 1936–39, Romanian & Hungarian 1941–45, Poland, Greece and Finland. Extend 335 Squadron and Finnish 20 provisional squadrons from original diaries. Include Coastal/FAA and Mediterranean/Scandinavia.
4. **All forces access queue:** NIMH, Belgian museum, Yugoslav, Slovak, RCAF, RAAF, RNZAF, SAAF, Bulgarian and Free French archives; record which require access, payment or in-person research. Do not fill uncovered forces with zero.
5. **Only then** design a true *Grand Air War* map/timeline: dated unit stationing, fronts and theaters; aircraft on hand vs ready; sortie and loss rates; toggle self-reported claims versus verified incidents; source fidelity always visible.

### Minimal audit checklist for future transcriptions

- The exact unit, parent, mission type, date/window and operational theater are explicit.
- The unit count, category count and grand total mathematically reconcile, or the source discrepancy is logged.
- Loss fate and cause are not guessed, enemy claims never substituted for independently verified losses.
- Original URL, scan page/folio when available, transcription revision and rights are recorded.
- The first usable source is not assumed to be complete: check where the personnel, reserve, naval, PVO, POW and support branches went.
- No sample or derivative is promoted to public charts until its unit aliases, definitions and chronology survive cross-source checking.
