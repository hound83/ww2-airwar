# Western air war in May and July 1944

The timeline extends from 30 June to 31 July. Its 1,980 dated records grow to 2,093. The additions comprise 31 Luftflotte 3 days, six fighter-type subtotals, 26 operation and formation observations, and 50 worksheet mission summaries. The target extract contains 497 underlying worksheet rows. The summaries and the case reports can cover the same aircraft.

## German loss returns

The July Luftflotte 3 return covers the Western Front reporting command and all aircraft types. Missing aircraft are part of enemy-action destruction. Air combat is another subset of enemy action. The daily capture retains an empty cell where the cause cannot be read reliably. The return provides the enemy-action destruction figure on every date after deriving 10 July from one missing aircraft and two destroyed in air combat. The sum equals the printed 602.

| Measure | Dates captured | Daily sum | Printed July total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Enemy action destroyed | 31 | 602 | 602 |
| Enemy action damaged | 26 | 107 | 126 |
| Other operational destroyed | 25 | 104 | 135 |
| Other operational damaged | 25 | 167 | 215 |
| Non-operational destroyed | 25 | 41 | 46 |
| Non-operational damaged | 25 | 100 | 116 |
| Missing, included in enemy action | 29 | 217 | 247 |
| Air combat destroyed, included in enemy action | 29 | 279 | 288 |
| Air combat damaged, included in enemy action | 25 | 71 | 87 |

The partial columns have outstanding cells on 9, 10, 16, 24, 25 and 30 July. In the 17 and 18 July rows, the table identifies single-engined Bf 109 and Fw 190 fighter subtotals. On 25 July it gives 33 enemy-action destructions including 11 missing and 22 destroyed in air combat across all types. The Bf 109 and Fw 190 single-engined fighter rows account for 15 and four of the 22. No individual German airframe is linked to an Eighth Air Force escort claim by this return. ## Davis workbook extraction

The Air University CD-ROM supplement supplies dated target observations rather than complete mission dispatch figures. Column G counts aircraft reported attacking. Under the AAF convention, lost aircraft and their bomb loads are included in the attacking force. Bomb weight uses U.S. short tons. The export preserves date, country, force, reported target, sighting method, attacking and lost counts, bomb types, total load, mission, area and the original spreadsheet row. Target spelling follows the worksheet.

| Worksheet | Date window | Country codes and rows | Distinct mission dates and numbers |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1944-1 | May | France 93, Belgium 23 | 22 |
| 1944-2 | July | France 163, Germany 208, Belgium 5, Netherlands 5 | 28 |

The extraction reviewed the first 1,000 indexed data rows of each workbook. It selected numbered Eighth Air Force missions with a positive attacking count and bomb load, a recorded loss cell, and a country code for France, Belgium, Germany or the Netherlands. The index reaches the end of the relevant France and Belgium May blocks but only the start of the January German block. May attacks in Germany need a separate read of the workbook beyond this window. July's visible block proceeds chronologically into August. Duplicate target names retain their original row numbers and are included in the mission summaries. The numbers do not establish complete RAF, Ninth or Fifteenth Air Force coverage.
