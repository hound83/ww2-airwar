Operation Bodenplatte

1 January 1945. German fighters struck Allied forward airfields in a bid to regain air superiority during the Ardennes offensive. At Eindhoven they caught aircraft lining up for takeoff. At Asch, two U.S. squadrons met the raid in the air. The five accounts below trace what happened to the aircraft and units involved.
