What the evidence does and does not say

The earlier preview showed whole-ETO USAAF group counts and command-wide German losses as prominent Normandy figures. Those populations are not Normandy-specific, so they have been removed from the campaign headline. Dated D-Day sorties, unit arrivals and researched landing-ground activity are shown only where a source supports them. Incomplete unit dates and conflicting field-opening conventions remain explicit.
