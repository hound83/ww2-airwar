Operation Husky · 1943

From Malta to Messina

Allied fighters covered the landings from offshore bases. By 12 July their attacks on Axis road traffic were helping the armies hold and expand their beachheads. From Pachino, No. 92 Squadron later covered Nelson and Rodney off Syracuse. At Messina, attacks on the ferries continued as Axis troops escaped to Italy.
