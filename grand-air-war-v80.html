RESEARCH LABORATORY 01

German aircraft losses: how, where, when?

These are captured German command-level reports, not aircraft shot down over an individual battle. Inspect the recorded destruction causes, damaged aircraft and reporting boundaries without inventing absent Eastern Front or Italian totals.

Chart bars are stacked into enemy action, other operational and non-operational. Missing aircraft are already contained in enemy-action destroyed; they are not added again. Gaps are blank, never zero.

Select a bar to inspect its original values.

OBSERVED RECORD

May 1944

April 1944 cross-check: Galland reported 489 fighter pilots lost and 396 replacement pilots. A separate scholarly casualty table reports 447 lost for April. Both are retained with their own definitions. Neither is presented as the number of aircraft destroyed.

EVIDENCE & OPEN QUESTIONS

Research is a feature, not a footnote.

The record exists only where a named wartime or official postwar publication establishes that observation. Each claim can be opened and checked. The Atlas also shows where we cannot yet establish a loss denominator or a historical unit position.

Historical borders are deliberately absent until a dated, source-validated 1936–1945 border series can be implemented. Terrain/coastlines are physical geography, not a wartime front-line reconstruction.
