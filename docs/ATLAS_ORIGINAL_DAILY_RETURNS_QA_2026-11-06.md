Norway: 28 May 1940 unit returns: 42 sorties by RAF No. 263 and 53 by RAF No. 46. The totals of 329 and 249 sorties belong to the later northern operational period, are not 28 May counts and are separate from the 49 April Lesjaskog flights. On 2 June, **both squadrons jointly** flew 75 sorties and fought 24 combat actions; neither is a German aircraft-loss number. These are based on the official RAF Air Historical Branch narrative citing original squadron ORBs; the entire Form 541 series has not been independently re-keyed.

Sicily: original HQ MAAF Feb 1945 table, **10 July–17 August**: 25,070 Allied sorties, 177 Allied aircraft losses, 293 Allied **claims** against enemy aircraft, 16,243 tons of bombs. None are single-day counts or the same population as the U.S. Navy's western landing-cover 440 sorties/day.
