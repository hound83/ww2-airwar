# Atlas roadmap after v10.0: who was there, who flew, and what changed?

The [active roadmap after v10.1](ATLAS_ROADMAP_AFTER_V10_1.md) combines all four regional investigations in v10.2, followed by the v11.0 cross-campaign comparisons. This document preserves the original coverage audit and the milestone shipped in v10.1.

**The v10.0 audit flagged a first priority: repair the way the Atlas represents deployed forces.** In the v10.0 Sicily explorer a reader could select just one German wing, SKG 10. The German return for 10 July puts four single-engine fighter Gruppen and two wing headquarters on Sicilian fields. SKG 10 belongs to the ground-attack section. The same version included just one of 23 squadrons in five named Commonwealth wings. Both sides need work. [Mediterranean source audit](../research/sources/deployment-audit/med.md)

A small number can also be correct. The German fighter return for mainland Italy on 20 August 1944 lists II./JG 77 alone, with 34 aircraft on strength and 26 serviceable. The same source lists Italian Republican fighters separately. At Bagration the Atlas already represents all five front-line Soviet air-army commands in the cited allocation. The policy below preserves narrow findings when the sources support them. [Mediterranean audit](../research/sources/deployment-audit/med.md) · [Eastern audit](../research/sources/deployment-audit/east.md)

**What this sweep delivers.** The [20-campaign assessment](../research/sources/atlas-coverage-audit.md) compares the public presentation with primary records and secondary histories. Its evidence pack includes [27 bounded populations](../research/transcriptions/deployment-benchmark-populations.csv), [163 named membership rows](../research/transcriptions/deployment-benchmark-members.csv), [139 figures classified by subject and measure](../research/transcriptions/dossier-fact-coverage.csv), the [reproducible baseline](../research/transcriptions/atlas-v10-coverage-inventory.csv) and [screen-level checks](../research/sources/deployment-audit/surfaces.md). v10.0 remains the audit baseline. **Milestone 1 shipped in v10.1.** All 20 dossiers now link to the [published rosters](../forces.html), with 44 scoped lists and 297 dated memberships. The [station audit](../research/sources/deployment-stations.md) records where competing accounts prevent a reliable pin. Milestone 2 is next.

## What the reader should see

A Sicily entry could read **“10 July 1943 · German single-engine fighters based on Sicily · 4 of 4 Gruppen named in this return, plus 2 wing headquarters · 136 aircraft held, 55 serviceable including headquarters.”** Opening a Gruppe would show its stations, source and any independently dated mission record. The Italian fighter return is dated 9 July. The wider Luftflotte 2 return also includes Sardinia and mainland Italy. The interface should show those choices before someone adds the figures.

| Evidence available | Display and counting rule |
|---|---|
| Source enumerates a bounded population | Give the scope, date and echelon. Show the matched identities divided by the expected identities. Link to both the list and the missing members. Distinguish “all identities named” from “each activity confirmed”. |
| Identifiable participants, strength return without names, or a gap in the reporting period | Use “at least N identified”, “N reported, identities unresolved”, or “no matching return located”. Give the next document needed. Empty filters require an explanation tied to the selected date and force. |
| Sources disagree | Retain their separate counts, definitions, dates and unit identities. Trace common upstream sources. Attribute the preferred interpretation only when the evidence resolves the difference. |
| Mixed hierarchy or changed subordination | Store parent, detachment, reporting command, service, role, base and effective dates. Count each unit once at the reader's chosen echelon. A headquarters, subordinate group, flight, replacement and returning sortie each has its own identity or event. |
| Operational effect | Link assigned → available/serviceable → flew → reached or engaged → reported effect only for the stages documented. A pilot's claim, an opponent's loss and a recipient's account retain their own source and time window. |

For every campaign, publish separate assessments of **formation identity, chronology, geography/bases, strength/readiness, effort by role, losses/damage and operational effect**. Show them by reporting side and relevant service. Compare the same geography and period before offering a percentage. A campaign-wide “percentage complete” would require a reliable historical denominator in every one of those dimensions. The v10.0 audit provides none at that breadth.
