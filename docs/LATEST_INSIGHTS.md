## Atlas v9.1

The January–June 1944 timeline contains 1,980 dated records. The June audit adds 60 all-aircraft command totals, 189 Bf 109 and Fw 190 role entries, and 12 formation, airframe and encounter entries. All 30 Luftflotte 3 daily totals reconcile with the printed monthly summary after deriving one blank subtotal from the individual rows. The Reich return covers all 30 days and retains three differences between the daily sum and printed monthly figures. The comparison distinguishes missing, air combat where captured, other operational loss and non-operational loss. [Timeline](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/operations-1944.html?preset=june) · [Source and mission audit](../research/sources/atlas-v91-june-loss-returns-and-missions-2026-11-12.md)

The six mission reviews add direct reporting on D-Day, the Munich escort and bomber losses, a possible Gelsenkirchen night-fighter match, and wider German losses on the Esternay and Cobra dates. The July command return supplies cause and outcome totals for Cobra. Identifying German airframes from the Mission 494 escort encounters still requires unit-level evidence. [Cobra evidence and estimate](https://hound83.github.io/ww2-airwar/grand-air-war-v80.html?loss-scope=cobra#claim-loss-calculator) · [Mission 494 archive lead](../research/sources/atlas-cobra-mission-494-attribution-2026-11-12.md)

Grok’s follow-up supplied the Cobra figures, the specialist Bodenplatte breakdown and the Davis and CAMO leads. The Atlas publishes the supported Cobra and German Bodenplatte observations. The Allied Bodenplatte totals still need reconciliation because the accessible accounts disagree about air and ground coverage.
